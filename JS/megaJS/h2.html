Who We Are

Winding Work Done Right, Every Time

At Aunshka Textile and Winding Works, we provide trusted winding and re-winding services tailored to each customer's requirements. Our focus is on delivering reliable, high-quality textile winding that meets exact specifications.

From yarn count 20 to 60, we handle a wide variety of winding tasks with the same care, precision, and professionalism that has earned us the trust of our clients over two decades.

We believe every cone wound correctly is a promise kept — to our customers, to quality, and to the craft of textile work.
